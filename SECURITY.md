## Security policy

Found a flaw? Write to junioad555@gmail.com with what you found and how to reproduce it. You will get
an acknowledgement within 3 business days; the full process is in
[docs/processo/resposta-a-vulnerabilidade.md](docs/processo/resposta-a-vulnerabilidade.md). Do not open
a public issue with exploitable details.
